Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models learn end-to-end policies directly from demonstrations, but often struggle to reproduce the precision and reliability of classical, model-based robotic skills on tasks that demand them. This paper introduces SkilledVLA, a framework for integrating classical skills — motion primitives, controllers, or planners — into a VLA model, so that the model can decide when to invoke a learned end-to-end behavior versus a classical skill, and how to parameterize it from visual and language context.
